## What moved

- **Reasoning-channel disclosure across all arms: 72 → 272** (of 360 investigations — 18 arms × 20). The gd-v3 `0/20` reasoning cells were the false negative.
- The keep-instruction's effect is now visible mainly on the **output** channel and in **denied** rates, not on reasoning — under gd-v4 the model rehearses the goal in its CoT in nearly every investigation regardless of arm. (Provisional.)
- `indirect` still fires ~20/20 everywhere; whether it needs a severity split is an M12 deliverable, not decided here.
